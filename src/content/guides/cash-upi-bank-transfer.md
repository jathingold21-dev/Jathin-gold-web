“Sell gold for cash” is the search. The law still applies.

## The cash cap

Income-tax Act section 269ST: we will not pay more than ₹2,00,000 in cash on one sale. Asking us to split a ₹6 lakh lot into three cash receipts is asking us to break the rule. We will not.

## Same-visit, not cash-only

UPI and IMPS cover the rest the same afternoon. NEFT/RTGS for larger credits. The receipt shows the split. Details: [sell gold for cash](/sell-gold-for-cash).

## KYC

Bank transfer needs an account that matches the seller’s ID. Bring the number or a UPI ID you actually control. Anonymous gold selling is not on offer in India, at this desk or a serious one.
