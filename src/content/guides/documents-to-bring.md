Indian gold buyers must know who they are paying. That is KYC, not nosiness.

## Always

A government photo ID. Aadhaar is the usual document. The name on the ID is the name on the receipt and, for bank transfer, should match the account.

## When the sale is ₹2,00,000 or more

PAN is required for that single transaction. If you do not have it with you, we cannot complete a large sale the same hour just because the gold is real.

## Optional

The original jewellery invoice. It can support a conversation about purity. It does not override the XRF reading. Family gold without a bill is still bought — identity of the seller is the requirement, not a 1998 receipt.

Cash on one sale cannot exceed ₹2 lakh ([payment guide](/guides/cash-upi-bank-transfer)). For the process itself see [how it works](/how-it-works).
