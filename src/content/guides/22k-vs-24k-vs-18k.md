Karat is a purity fraction. 24K is treated as fine gold (999). 22K is 916 parts gold per thousand. 18K is 750. The indicative 22K rate on our [gold rate](/gold-rate-today) page is the 24K figure multiplied by 0.916.

## What the stamp is worth

A 22K hallmark is a strong hint. It is not a laboratory certificate for a chain that has been soldered twice. We still XRF. If the gun reads 21.2K, we do not pay 22K because the inside of the clasp says 22.

## Coins and jewellery

Coins and bars are usually 24K or 999. Jewellery in this part of India is usually 22K. Imported and machine-made pieces are often 18K. Mixed lots are split by reading, not by the prettiest piece in the pouch.

Use the [calculator](/calculator) with the karat you believe you have, then expect the desk to confirm or correct it.
