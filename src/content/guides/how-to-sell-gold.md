A jewellery store that buys gold is usually trying to sell you something else. A dedicated buyer is trying to finish a purchase. That difference shows up in how long you wait, whether the test happens in front of you, and whether the quote is cash or “value against a new set.”

## Before you leave home

Check a live 22K figure so you have a band, not a fantasy number. Weigh the lot roughly if you can. Put the pieces in one pouch. Take Aadhaar. Take PAN if you think the payout will cross ₹2 lakh.

## At the desk

The sequence should be boring: weigh, XRF, written quote, KYC, pay. If any step happens out of sight, stop. If the quote is only verbal, stop. That is the sequence we use at the Kothagudem desk. We are available in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

## After you accept

You should leave with a receipt that shows weight, purity, rate, deductions, and the payment mode. Keep it. Gold sales are not anonymous in India.
