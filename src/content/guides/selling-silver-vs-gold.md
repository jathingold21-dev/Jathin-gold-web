Ten grams of 22K gold is a household decision. Ten grams of silver is a spoon. That ratio is why people are surprised by how much silver they must carry for a meaningful payout.

## Purity

Gold jewellery clusters. Silver does not. Sterling 92.5, fine 999, and mixed household lots all show up in the same bag. Oxidisation does not remove silver, but solder and copper do change the reading. See [sell silver](/sell-silver) and the [silver rate](/silver-rate-today).

## Speed

The steps are identical to gold: weigh, XRF, quote, KYC, pay. Large silver lots take longer on the scale, not on the paperwork.

If you have both metals, we still do one visit. Gold and silver are priced separately on the same receipt.
