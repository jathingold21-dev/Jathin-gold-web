A written gold quote is the slip you can read before any piece leaves your hand. A spoken number is not a quote.

## What belongs on the slip

- Gross weight, in grams
- The purity the XRF actually reported, not only the karat stamped on a clasp
- The rate per gram used for that purity
- Any deduction, named, with its amount
- The rupee figure you will receive, and whether it is cash, UPI, or a bank transfer

If a line is missing, ask for it before you say yes. Weigh, test, write the quote, then pay — that is the sequence we follow at the Kothagudem desk.

## What to compare

Bring your own ballpark from the [calculator](/calculator) or the [gold rate today](/gold-rate-today) page. The written quote will not match a jewellery-store board. Those boards price ornaments for sale. A buying desk prices the metal it can hedge, after the test, on the day.

## When to stop

Stop if the test happens in another room. Stop if the only number is verbal. Stop if the rate is for 24K and your piece tested as 22K, with no line showing the difference.

When the slip is complete, payment is the last step. [Contact the desk](/contact) if you want the quote done in front of you.
