Traditional gold testing often involved touchstone scratching and nitric acid, or destructive melting before giving you a valuation. At modern gold evaluation desks, German XRF Karatmeters have replaced these outdated methods entirely.

## How XRF Testing Works

X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectroscopy works by directing a low-intensity X-ray beam at the surface of your gold jewellery. Each elemental component in the alloy (gold, silver, copper, zinc, nickel) emits fluorescent X-rays at characteristic energy levels.

The detector measures these energy signatures and calculates the precise elemental composition down to parts per thousand in less than 60 seconds.

## Advantages for Gold Sellers

1. **Zero Weight Loss**: No rubbing on a touchstone, no acid scraping, and no melting loss.
2. **Transparent Readout**: The digital screen displays the exact karatage (e.g. 22K / 91.6% or 18K / 75.0%) directly in front of you.
3. **No Obligation**: Because your jewellery remains completely intact, you are free to accept the valuation or take your pieces home without any compromise.
