Gold rates in Hyderabad change daily according to international bullion movements, currency exchange rates, and national import duties. Understanding how these factors impact your payout ensures you get fair market value.

## International Spot vs Domestic Landing Price

International gold trades in US Dollars per troy ounce. When bullion enters India, basic customs duty, agricultural cess, and landing factors are added. This creates the domestic base price for 24K pure gold.

## Translating 24K to 22K Jewellery Payout

Most wearable jewellery in South India is crafted in 22K (91.6% purity) or 18K (75.0% purity). The transparent payout calculation is straightforward:

$$\text{22K Base Rate} = \text{24K Live Rate} \times \frac{22}{24} \ (\approx 91.67\%)$$

A genuine precious metal buyer benchmarks directly against this live figure and writes the exact calculation on your slip without deducting hidden melting charges or arbitrary retail margins.
